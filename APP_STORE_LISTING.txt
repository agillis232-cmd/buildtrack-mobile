Full Description:
BuildTrack Pro is the all-in-one construction management platform built for residential remodelers and general contractors.

Manage every project from first estimate to final punch list — specs, schedules, change orders, invoicing, daily logs, and client communication — all in one app.

BUILT FOR THE FIELD
- Take and organize site photos directly from your phone
- Create daily logs with crew size, hours, and materials
- Scan receipts with AI to auto-categorize expenses
- View project schedules and track progress on the go

PROJECT MANAGEMENT
- Full spec book system — track every material, finish, and fixture by room
- Change orders with digital signatures and cost breakdowns
- RFIs with automatic PM notifications
- Punch lists to close out projects cleanly

FINANCIAL TOOLS
- Estimates with payment schedules and draw requests
- Invoice creation and payment tracking
- Vendor bill management with lien waiver tracking
- P&L reports and financial dashboards

CLIENT & SUB PORTALS
- Clients view progress, approve change orders, and see their schedule
- Subcontractors manage compliance docs, submit RFIs, and log daily work
- Subs can work with multiple general contractors from one account

TEAM COLLABORATION
- Invite team members with role-based access
- Share documents and photos across your organization
- Real-time project updates for everyone involved
